Log a set and the rest timer starts from restEndsAt, an epoch timestamp, derived on every render — never a decrementing counter, which drifts or freezes exactly when the phone locks. Every change writes through to storage: reload the page mid-session and nothing is lost. You're not following a program yet.

Workount tracks two different things, and keeps them apart on purpose:

  1. 01A program is the plan — the days you intend to train and what each one prescribes.
  2. 02A session is what actually happened. Editing the plan next month never rewrites it.

Build the first one and the app will tell you what to train today.

Empty state — no active program The one place the app is allowed to use sentences. It teaches the single distinction the whole product rests on, then gets out of the way.

Colour

Restrained: the signal never exceeds ~5% of pixels. Every ratio below is computed, not estimated — and both themes are designed, not derived. Switch the theme above and watch the whole ramp change rather than invert.

Type

IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono — one superfamily, paired grotesque-against-monospace rather than two sans faces that clash by being nearly alike. Plex was drawn for engineering contexts, and it keeps the app off Inter, which would drag it straight back to the dashboard look we're avoiding.
